## Summary

Before, a long menu-bar toolbar can run into the Dynamic Island while a Live Activity is visible. On smaller MacBook displays, the toolbar text is clipped abruptly, with no fade or other indication that content continues beyond the available width.

After, toolbar content approaching the Dynamic Island would fade or blur toward the obstruction instead of ending with a hard cutoff. The change should preserve normal menu-bar behavior when the island is absent and should be verified with menu-heavy applications such as Keynote, including different display widths and Dynamic Island activity states.
