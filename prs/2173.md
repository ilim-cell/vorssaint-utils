## Summary

Before, Dynamic Island volume and brightness sliders require a precise click-and-drag, which is difficult on the narrow control.

After, a two-finger horizontal trackpad swipe would adjust the active slider while ordinary mouse click-and-drag remains supported. The input-routing design must distinguish slider adjustment from page navigation, define sensitivity and bounds, and preserve accessibility and multi-display behavior before implementation.
