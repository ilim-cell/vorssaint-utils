## Summary

Before, Dynamic Island navigation relies primarily on point-and-click controls.

After, gestures such as a two-finger downward swipe to open the panel and a two-finger horizontal swipe between pages would provide a more direct trackpad interaction. Gesture precedence, conflicts with slider gestures and system gestures, cancellation thresholds, reduced motion, and VoiceOver behavior must be defined before implementation.
