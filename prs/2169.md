## Summary

Before, a live activity can expand the visible Dynamic Island beyond its normal notch footprint, but clicks in that expanded area may be delivered to the desktop instead of reopening the island.

After, the full visible activity region would accept the same open interaction as the regular notch area, including the timer icon and clock. The fix needs to account for display coordinates, animation-time geometry, and overlapping menu-bar hit testing; a reproducible interaction test is required before implementation.
